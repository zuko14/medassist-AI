💬 Patient Follow-ups

A message sent automatically after a patient's visit — the thing that makes this more than a booking bot. Visits are closed out automatically each night, so nobody has to mark anything.

1 = the next day. Retried for 3 days if WhatsApp refuses the first attempt.

A follow-up always arrives outside WhatsApp's 24-hour reply window, so Meta only allows an approved template. To send the wording above, get a template approved with two variables — {{1}} the patient's first name and {{2}} your message — and put its name here. Leave this empty and the built-in follow-up is sent instead; your custom wording will not be delivered.
