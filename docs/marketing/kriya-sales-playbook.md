## 2. Discovery Framework & High-Impact Questions

Never open a meeting by talking about AI features. Open with operational diagnostics:

1. **Question 1 (Front-Desk Volume):** *"Doctor / Administrator, between 9 AM and 12 PM, how many phone calls does your reception desk receive from patients asking about doctor timings, fees, or slot availability?"*
   * *Target realization:* 40–80 calls/day; receptionist is constantly interrupted while attending to in-person patients.
2. **Question 2 (No-Show Friction):** *"When patients book appointments over a phone call, what percentage of them fail to show up or arrive 45 minutes late without informing you?"*
   * *Target realization:* 20%–35% no-show rate; represents direct daily revenue loss.
3. **Question 3 (Diagnostic Labs):** *"How much staff time is spent downloading PDF test results, searching patient contacts, and sending them manually over WhatsApp?"*
   * *Target realization:* 1–2 full hours of administrative labor daily; high risk of misdirected reports.

## 3. Comprehensive Customer Objection Map & Defenses

```

|                               OBJECTION HANDLING & DEFENSE MANUAL                                  |

```

### Objection 1: "We already use Practo / Practo Ray."
* **Root Concern:** Prospect feels they already have software that handles appointments.
* **Factual Sales Defense:**
  > *"Practo is a fantastic patient discovery marketplace, and you should keep using it to acquire new patients. But Practo is built to promote its own platform—patients searching for you are often shown nearby competitor clinics. Kriya AI is different: it is your clinic's own private operating layer on your dedicated WhatsApp number. When your existing patients, referrals, or Google searchers message you, Kriya AI books them directly into your schedule with your branding, zero marketplace commissions, and automatic live queue tracking."*

### Objection 2: "We already use MocDoc / HMIS for our hospital."
* **Root Concern:** Reluctance to rip and replace their existing core hospital software.
* **Factual Sales Defense:**
  > *"We do not replace MocDoc—we supercharge it! MocDoc is an excellent back-office system for inpatient billing and pharmacy, but your patients don't have access to MocDoc on their phones. Kriya AI acts as the modern WhatsApp front desk for MocDoc. Through our automated browser and API connectors, appointments booked by patients on WhatsApp sync directly into your MocDoc schedule, and lab reports generated in MocDoc are automatically delivered to patients on WhatsApp with zero manual staff effort."*

### Objection 3: "Our receptionist already handles WhatsApp messages manually."
* **Root Concern:** Reluctance to spend money when staff is already doing the task.
* **Factual Sales Defense:**
  > *"When your receptionist is manually typing on WhatsApp at 10:30 AM, there are patients standing in front of the desk waiting to be checked in, bills waiting to be settled, and the clinic landline is ringing. Human receptionists take 3 to 10 minutes to negotiate a slot via text. Kriya AI handles 20 patient inquiries simultaneously in under 60 seconds—even at 11 PM when your clinic is closed. Your staff can focus entirely on delivering a great in-person patient experience."*

### Objection 4: "We don't need AI. AI makes medical mistakes."
* **Root Concern:** Fear of medical malpractice liability under National Medical Commission (NMC) regulations.
* **Factual Sales Defense:**
  > *"You are 100% right to be cautious, and that is exactly why Kriya AI is engineered differently. Kriya AI never diagnoses illnesses or prescribes medicine. We have built a deterministic 'Clinical Safety Firewall' that screens over 100 Indian drug names and dosage queries. If a patient asks 'What medicine should I take for fever?', the system instantly blocks medical advice and safely redirects them to book a consultation with you. AI is used solely to understand languages (English, Hindi, Telugu) and schedule appointments fast."*

### Objection 5: "Our patients are older or not tech-savvy; they prefer to call."
* **Root Concern:** Skepticism regarding patient digital adoption.
* **Factual Sales Defense:**
  > *"That is the beauty of WhatsApp—over 500 million Indians of all age groups already use WhatsApp daily to talk to their family. They don't have to download an app, remember passwords, or create an account. If an elderly patient simply types 'Doctor appointment tomorrow', Kriya AI responds instantly in their language. And for family members managing care for elderly parents, our built-in dependent booking makes scheduling effortless."*

### Objection 6: "What happens if a patient's payment fails or WhatsApp is down?"
* **Root Concern:** Operational fragility and transaction loss.
* **Factual Sales Defense:**
  > *"Kriya AI uses bank-grade Razorpay webhooks with automated 10-minute temporary slot reservation holds. If payment is completed, the slot is instantly confirmed and locked. If the payment link expires, the slot is automatically released back to the public pool so no revenue is lost. If Meta WhatsApp experiences a regional outage, all clinic appointments remain safely visible and editable in your staff web admin dashboard."*

### Objection 7: "Is patient data safe? What about the DPDP Act 2023?"
* **Root Concern:** Legal compliance and data privacy penalties.
* **Factual Sales Defense:**
  > *"Kriya AI is designed specifically for India's DPDP Act 2023 and NMC requirements. Every patient is shown an explicit data consent prompt on first interaction. Furthermore, our database implements strict multi-tenant Row-Level Security (RLS) ensuring your clinic's patient records are completely isolated and never shared. We also provide automated right-to-erasure and 7-year compliant audit retention logs."*

### Objection 8: "Why shouldn't we hire another receptionist for ₹12,000/month instead?"
* **Root Concern:** Evaluating software subscription vs. additional human labor.
* **Factual Sales Defense:**
  > *"A human receptionist costs ₹12,000 to ₹15,000/month, works 8 hours a day, takes leave on Sundays/holidays, can only answer one phone call at a time, and makes human scheduling errors. Kriya AI costs ₹2,499 to ₹5,999/month, works 24/7/365, handles unlimited simultaneous patient bookings instantly, sends automated 24h reminders, and never forgets a doctor's leave."*
