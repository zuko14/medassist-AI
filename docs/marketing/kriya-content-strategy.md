## 1. The Core Objective: Qualified Healthcare Leads vs. Vanity Metrics

Kriya AI’s content strategy is strictly engineered to generate **high-intent, qualified healthcare commercial inquiries (Clinic Owners, Managing Doctors, Hospital Administrators, Diagnostic Lab Heads)** rather than hollow viral engagement. 

Every published article, infographic, carousel, and video must answer one core question for a healthcare operator:
> *"How does this solve my front-desk chaos, reduce my operational overhead, and protect my clinical reputation?"*

## 3. Platform-Specific Channel Strategy

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                               CHANNEL ARCHITECTURE & CONTENT MATRIX                                |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 LinkedIn (Primary B2B Credibility & Inbound Channel)
* **Audience:** Hospital Directors, Managing Doctors, Healthcare Consultants, Hospital CIOs/CTOs, Practice Managers.
* **Content Formats:**
  * High-value PDF carousels (e.g., *"The Anatomy of a 60-Second Hospital Front Desk"*).
  * Long-form architectural & operational case studies.
  * Technical explainers on DPDP Act 2023 and NMC clinical safety in healthtech.
  * Short 45-second screen recording demos showing real WhatsApp flows and admin dashboards.
* **Cadence:** 3 to 4 high-substance posts per week.

### 3.2 YouTube (Deep Demonstrations & Technical Authority)
* **Audience:** Clinic owners evaluating software, hospital IT committees, procurement officers.
* **Content Formats:**
  * 3-minute to 8-minute comprehensive product walkthroughs (e.g., *"How Kriya AI Connects to Your Hospital's Existing Doctor Roster"*).
  * 60-second YouTube Shorts featuring focused single-problem teardowns (e.g., *"Why 24-Hour WhatsApp Reminders Slash Clinic No-Shows"*).
* **Cadence:** 1 Long-form video bi-weekly + 2 Shorts per week.

### 3.3 Instagram (Visual Authority, Clinic Culture & Short-Form Video)
* **Audience:** Younger specialist doctors, dental clinic chains, aesthetic/dermatology clinic owners, clinic coordinators.
* **Content Formats:**
  * 9:16 High-production UI motion Reels (e.g., *"A Patient's Journey: From WhatsApp 'Hi' to Verified Doctor Slot in 58 Seconds"*).
  * Clean carousel templates contrasting *"Manual Front Desk vs. Kriya AI Operating Layer"*.
* **Cadence:** 3 Reels + 2 Carousel posts per week.

### 3.4 X / Twitter (Engineering Depth & Industry Commentary)
* **Audience:** Healthtech founders, developers, healthcare technology analysts, tech-forward clinicians.
* **Content Formats:**
  * Engineering threads on distributed locking, multi-tenant PostgreSQL RLS, and zero-LLM regex firewalls.
  * Commentary on ABDM policies, DPDP data governance, and AI safety in healthcare.
* **Cadence:** 2 to 3 insightful threads per week.

### 3.5 Direct WhatsApp Business Broadcasts (Nurturing & Client Onboarding)
* **Audience:** Verified demo attendees, pilot clinic staff, and opted-in doctors.
* **Content Formats:**
  * Product update highlights, operational best-practice tips for front desks, new integration announcements.
* **Cadence:** 1 bi-weekly high-value utility update (strict opt-in compliance).

## 5. Factual Claim Verification & Content Governance Guidelines

To maintain absolute credibility with medical professionals, every content piece must adhere to strict verification standards:

1. **Rule of Traceability:** Never publish a percentage, uptime claim, or operational metric that is not verified in internal testing or cited from recognized industry research (NMC, NHA, MoHFW, Meta API documentation).
2. **Zero Medical Claims:** Content must explicitly avoid claiming diagnostic or therapeutic capabilities. All communications must reinforce that Kriya AI is an **administrative and operational layer**, not a medical practitioner.
3. **Synthetic Demo Data Only:** Video recordings, UI mockups, and screenshots must exclusively utilize synthetic, test clinic environments (`City Care Hospital`, `Dr. Arjun Reddy`, `MC-2026-XXXX`). Never expose real patient names, phone numbers, or clinical records.
