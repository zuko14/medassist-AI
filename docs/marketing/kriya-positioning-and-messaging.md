## 1. Core Category Definition

**Category Name:** **The WhatsApp Hospital Operating Layer (Hospital Front-Desk Automation SaaS)**

Kriya AI is neither a generic conversational chatbot nor a bulky back-office EMR. It is the **autonomous operational layer** that connects the patient's natural communication channel (WhatsApp) directly with the hospital’s doctors, schedules, billing, waiting room queues, and diagnostic laboratories.

> **Patients can pay consultation fees via integrated UPI/Razorpay links, receive automated 24-hour and 2-hour reminders that reduce no-shows to near zero, track their live queue token from home or the waiting area, and receive their diagnostic lab reports with clear, patient-friendly summaries.**

> **For hospital management, Kriya AI provides a secure multi-branch dashboard, staff role-based access control, automated doctor leave rescheduling, and non-invasive browser connectors that sync seamlessly with legacy EMRs like MocDoc. It is 100% DPDP Act 2023 and NMC compliant, delivering immediate operational relief and measurable revenue recovery from Day 1."**

## 5. Persona-Specific Messaging & Value Propositions

### 5.1 Clinic Owner / Senior Consultant Doctor
* **Core Concern:** Protecting their practice reputation, reducing administrative chaos, and avoiding no-shows.
* **Pitch Hook:** *"You spent 15 years building your clinical reputation. Don't let a chaotic front desk and missed phone calls cost you patients."*
* **Key Value Props:**
  * Automated WhatsApp booking on your clinic's own brand.
  * Optional pre-consultation UPI deposit to guarantee patient commitment.
  * Respects your exact shift hours, buffer timings, and emergency leave schedules.
  * Clinical Safety Firewall ensures your liability is 100% protected under NMC rules.

### 5.2 Hospital Managing Director / Chief Operating Officer (COO)
* **Core Concern:** OPD throughput, multi-branch visibility, staff productivity, patient satisfaction scores (CSAT).
* **Pitch Hook:** *"Scale your hospital's outpatient volume across all city branches without adding a single reception desk employee."*
* **Key Value Props:**
  * Centralized multi-branch dashboard governing doctor rosters, patient queues, and payments.
  * Live waiting room queue management decreases crowded reception areas and patient frustration.
  * Non-invasive browser connectors integrate with your existing HMIS without expensive developer contracts.
  * Comprehensive analytics tracking patient acquisition channels, peak rush hours, and doctor utilization.

### 5.3 Diagnostic Center / Pathology Lab Owner
* **Core Concern:** High staffing costs for report printing/dispatch, delayed turnaround times, patient inquiry calls.
* **Pitch Hook:** *"Deliver verified diagnostic test reports to patients on WhatsApp the second they are approved—with zero manual staff forwarding."*
* **Key Value Props:**
  * Automated PDF text extraction and OCR pipeline delivering reports directly to the patient's verified mobile number.
  * AI-generated plain-language summaries help patients understand their results before doctor review.
  * Automatic matching of lab reports with booking references eliminates misdirected medical data.
  * Eliminates front-desk congestion from patients queuing just to collect physical paper envelopes.

### 5.4 Front-Desk Receptionist / Clinic Coordinator
* **Core Concern:** Exhaustion from answering the same phone inquiries all day while dealing with angry walk-in queues.
* **Pitch Hook:** *"Let Kriya AI handle the 100 repetitive WhatsApp inquiries about timings and slots so you can focus on welcoming patients."*
* **Key Value Props:**
  * 80% reduction in phone inquiries regarding hospital timings, fees, and doctor availability.
  * 1-click check-in assigns unique queue tokens that patients track directly on their phones.
  * Instant access to doctor leave updates and emergency cancellations with automated patient rescheduling.

### 5.5 Hospital CTO / Chief Information Officer (CIO)
* **Core Concern:** Data security, multi-tenancy, uptime reliability, regulatory compliance, API standards.
* **Pitch Hook:** *"An enterprise-grade, multi-tenant healthcare operating layer built on FastAPI, Supabase RLS, FHIR R4, and HMAC-verified Meta Cloud Webhooks."*
* **Key Value Props:**
  * Strict PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) ensuring absolute tenant and patient data isolation.
  * Standardized HL7 FHIR R4 REST endpoints (`/fhir/Patient`, `/fhir/Appointment`, `/fhir/DiagnosticReport`).
  * DPDP Act 2023 consent lifecycle and NMC 7-year tiered data retention automation.
  * 438 verified automated test suites covering race conditions, payment idempotency, and distributed locks.

## 6. Brand Voice & Communication Style Guide

| Attribute | How Kriya AI Communicates | What Kriya AI Strictly Avoids |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tone** | Technically serious, operationally precise, respectful, and reassuring. | Flippant, overly casual, or breathless "Silicon Valley AI magic" hype. |
| **Clinical Position** | Clear operational boundary: *"We automate hospital administration and triage; doctors provide medical care."* | Making clinical diagnoses, suggesting drug dosages, or claiming "AI doctor" capabilities. |
| **Language** | Plain, professional, and accessible in English, Hindi, and Telugu. | Opaque medical jargon or dense developer buzzwords when talking to clinics. |
| **Honesty** | Evidence-backed claims grounded in verifiable software capabilities and actual tests. | Unsubstantiated claims like "100% error-free", "zero wait time", or fabricated ROI percentages. |
